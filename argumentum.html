Press Kit

Programme note

Burnin' Chamber Music (!) is a work in two parts for alto saxophone, baritone saxophone, unfretted bass and junk percussion. A rough, lurching and lyrical sound. The writing is formal, taut, and leaves spaces where improvisation will rush in.

Biography

Comodoro Süleyman is a musician, composer and draughtsman. He has developed a practice as bassist and improviser, from chanson française to Colombian cumbia. He has played with Margriet and John Tchicai, Raul de Souza, Daniel Garbin, Jackson Scott Walter, Bombes 2 bal and La Macchina Volante. He also collaborates as a graphic artist with Los Gusanos (Bristol, Ninja Tune). Burnin' Chamber Music (!) is his first project as a composer.

Short version

Burnin' Chamber Music (!) is a composed work for small ensemble, alto or tenor saxophone, baritone saxophone, bass and junk percussion, carried by improvisers. The architecture is rigorous, the writing tight, but the music burns and consumes itself in a blind dance*.

* Hard hat recommended.
